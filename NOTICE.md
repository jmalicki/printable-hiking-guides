# Third-party material

This repository contains material that belongs to others. It keeps its own terms.

- **Trail data** (`data/*/osm.json`, and the routes, junctions and GPX files computed from it, including the driving
  routes from OSRM): © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).
- **Basemaps** (`data/*/*basemap.png`): USGS The National Map, public domain. Elevation: USGS 3DEP, public domain,
  through [opentopodata.org](https://www.opentopodata.org/).
- **Photographs** (`data/*/photos/`, and as embedded in the built guides under `out/`): see [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)
  for each photo's author, licence and source. Several are licensed for non-commercial use only, and some may not be
  altered.

The guides are compiled from maps and published sources. Check conditions, rules and closures with the land manager
before relying on one.
